# Persona Corpus Harvest

## Donor

`Persona’s.zip`

Observed corpus: 57 files, including 18 completed persona documents with Markdown/PDF counterparts, research notes, one incomplete Tony Robbins research file, collection index, and TODO material.

## Authority

`DONOR_EVIDENCE_ONLY`

The pack is useful for structural persona intelligence. It is **not** promoted as evidence-grade biography. Several persona documents contain placeholder citation ranges and unsupported completeness/word-count claims.

## Repeated architecture harvested

Across the completed persona documents, the recurring semantic structure includes:

- principles/worldview,
- decision-making frameworks,
- communication/presentation behavior,
- domain frameworks,
- contextual adaptation,
- interaction/disagreement/collaboration behavior,
- implementation guidance,
- conformance/quality guidance,
- multi-persona/round-table behavior in many documents.

## High-value primitives

1. `DerivedPersona`
2. `PrincipleSet`
3. `CognitiveProfile`
4. `DecisionLens`
5. `DomainLens`
6. `PresentationProfile`
7. `InteractionProfile`
8. `AdaptationPolicy`
9. component-level persona provenance
10. persona conformance evals
11. persona compilation/minimization

## Rejected donor behavior

- treating public-figure persona prose as verified biography,
- source-person identity simulation as architecture,
- prompt files as canonical persona authority,
- full-dossier injection as runtime hydration,
- unsupported references as evidence,
- persona traits granting operational authority.

## Disposition

Harvest semantics into IdentityOS Persona architecture. Preserve donor files as provenance/evidence only; do not install the persona corpus as canonical runtime state.
