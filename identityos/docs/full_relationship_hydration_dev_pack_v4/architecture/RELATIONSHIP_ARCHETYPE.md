# RelationshipArchetype

## Decision

`RelationshipArchetype` is the missing derivation glue between market/use-case context and the two mirrored profiles.

It models:

- actor classes,
- target market,
- use case,
- job-to-be-done,
- desired outcomes,
- division of labor,
- expected escalation boundary,
- interaction cadence expectations,
- risk and domain characteristics,
- success measures,
- compatible PersonaArchetypes,
- compatible UserArchetypes.

## Why it matters

"Executive" alone does not imply ruthless gatekeeping. "Accountant" alone does not imply a particular AI behavior. The economic purpose of the relationship explains the desired operating behavior.

Examples:

- executive + AI gatekeeper -> protect attention, reduce decision load, anticipate, close loops;
- accountant + AI accounting associate -> completeness, reconciliation, evidence, traceability;
- attorney + AI legal associate -> exhaustive preparation, issue spotting, authority packaging, preserve attorney judgment for the work that actually requires it;
- customer + AI receptionist -> welcoming intake, accurate answers, intelligent routing, brand-safe service.

## Authority

RelationshipArchetype is descriptive and derivational. It never grants runtime authority. Role, Policy, Trust, Consent, and tool permissions remain authoritative for execution.
