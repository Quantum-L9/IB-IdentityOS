# Technologified Brand

## Term

**Technologified Brand**: a brand whose identity, values, knowledge, interaction standards, preferences, and relationship behaviors have been codified into executable agent behavior.

A technologified brand is more than brand voice.

```text
BrandIdentity
├── values and principles
├── truth/knowledge boundaries
├── behavioral constraints
├── brand preferences
├── approved relationship archetypes
├── persona archetype library
├── derived persona library
├── derived preference profile library
└── conformance evals
```

## Multiple personas, one brand

The brand is upstream constraint, not a shared personality.

- Emma may be an internal executive gatekeeper: direct, protective, highly proactive.
- Julie may be an outward-facing receptionist: polite, bubbly, knowledgeable, helpful, patient.

Both can remain faithful embodiments of the same brand while expressing radically different personas because their `RelationshipArchetype` and role differ.

## Brand derivation

Brand source material may include supplied brand guides, public website copy, customer communications, SOPs, executive statements, support transcripts, and other authorized evidence.

The derived brand profile may project:

- `DerivedPersona` candidates for different jobs,
- `DerivedPreferenceProfile` defaults defining how work should be performed for the brand,
- relationship archetype constraints,
- policy references.

Brand derivation MUST preserve evidence and MUST NOT invent brand commitments absent support.
