## Preference corpus disposition

The historical preference files prove that preference semantics included communication, workflow, output, reasoning, scope, strength, corrections, indexing, and persistence concerns. They also demonstrate the failure of collapsing all those concerns into one universal root kernel.

Accepted: structured preferences, correction evidence, drift/supersession, relationship specificity, preference-aware behavior.

Rejected: monolithic universal preference authority, preference-as-embedding, fixed arbitrary confidence, direct persistence bypasses, and deprecated prompt-loader machinery.

## Persona corpus disposition

The public-figure persona corpus remains structural donor evidence only. It contributes DecisionLens/component decomposition and provenance lessons. Unsupported biography or endorsement claims are not canonical IdentityOS truth.

## LCTO failure disposition

The LCTO cold-start experience is treated as operator-supplied product evidence that agent-only hydration can fail the subjective and operational "MY agent" objective even when the agent was intentionally designed.
